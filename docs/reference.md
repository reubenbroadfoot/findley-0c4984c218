## Systems

- **ParentSquare** — the primary school-to-home channel. Nearly every signup and weather alert runs through it. Accounts auto-created from ParentVUE contacts. This matters more than the website.
- **ParentVUE** — enrollment, grades, contacts, bus assignment (refreshes Saturdays 3:30 am), teacher messaging.
- **Raptor** — volunteer background check. Up to 10 business days, valid two years, unique email per volunteer, government photo ID at every visit.
- **SchoolCafé** — meal accounts, schoolcafe.com/beavertonsd. Apply for benefits regardless of income; Oregon's Expanded Income Guidelines reach 300% of the federal poverty level.

## Programs

- **TAG** — universal screening in grades 3, 4, 5. Grade 4 takes the NGAT-Verbal, targeted for completion within October. Parent referral form exists but is not required. **The district does not accept private testing.** Identification is permanent; retesting every two years.
- **OBOB** — grades 3–5. Teams of 4–5, students recruit their own teammates, medieval-themed name. Registration opens September 2026; battles late January 2027.
- **Dragon Riders** — apply in spring of 4th grade for trimester 1 of 5th. 30 seats, lottery.
- **Math Olympiad** — 4th–5th, monthly contests November through March.
- **Passport Club** — grades 2–5, monthly Wednesday checks October to May. 4th grade works Levels 1–4.
- **Findley Flyers** — K–5, automatic membership, free. Tuesdays at recess, Thursdays after school.
- **Science Fair** — K–5, mid-March, proposals late February.
- **Chess Club** — Mon beginner / Wed intermediate, 2:55–3:45 pm, Room 201, 30 per session. Grades and cost have never been published.
- **4th grade health** — *The Great Body Shop* includes "Hormones and Body Changes" and "Hormones and Reproductive Organs." Opt out of sexuality-education components by emailing the teacher and principal naming the specific lessons.
